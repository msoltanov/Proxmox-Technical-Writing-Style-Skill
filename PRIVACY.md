# Privacy Policy

This project is a skill: Markdown instructions, manifests, and images. It has no server, no MCP server, no hooks, and no analytics.

The project does not collect, store, or send personal data.

When you use the skill, your AI tool reads the skill files. Your text goes only to the AI tool and its provider. The privacy policy of that provider applies to your text.

For a question about this policy, open an issue:

https://github.com/msoltanov/Proxmox-Technical-Writing-Style-Skill/issues
